# P25 — beta.11: tips that teach over time

Status: **approved** (2026-10-28), with knowledge graded rather than switched (below). Builds on beta.10's Help page,
gestures registry and badges (P24.6).

## Why

The brief (item 6.2–6.7, beta.10 round): someone who found the repo and
installed the app can find their way around Studio, but does not know how
the game plays; nobody finds out by themselves that questions can be dragged
onto a domain, that Ctrl turns the drag into a move, that Shift-click selects
a run. Help (beta.10) answers anyone who goes looking. Tips reach the ones
who don't.

**Not a tutorial.** No sequence of steps that must be clicked through. A
**progressive tutorial over time**: one tip at a time, when what the operator
is doing makes it relevant, more often for someone new, fewer and fewer as
they show they know the app — and never one for something they already do.
Local and offline, like everything else.

## Goals / non-goals / anti-goals

- **Goals:** the right tip at the right moment (the editor of a first
  question says *make a domain first*; back on the list after the first save
  says *import many at once, with the AI template*); fewer tips as experience
  grows; a tip less likely each time the operator shows they know it (they
  used the gesture), and in practice gone once they use it often; visual tips (keys, gestures, labels, small figures) — the
  badges Help already draws.
- **Non-goals:** a step-by-step onboarding; tips on Stage or in the
  Statistics window; tips in Romanian (Studio's UI is English; What's new
  stays EN+RO); telemetry — nothing leaves the machine.
- **Anti-goals:** a tip during a show (§6.6: none while a Play is playing or
  paused, unless it is a **rehearsal** — one team); dimming or a focus trap
  (§6.7); a tip that steals focus or keys; a tip twice in a row, or the same
  tip forever.

## How it decides (the engine)

A pure module, `apps/studio/src/tips/engine.ts`, tested without React, with a
seeded random source so every decision is reproducible in a test.

**Moments.** Studio reports what just happened as a *moment* — a small
vocabulary, not free text: `route:<tab>` (arrived on a tab), `editor:new-question`,
`question:saved`, `questions:selected-many`, `game:opened`, `control:loaded`, `control:rehearsal`,
`displays:opened`, `first-launch`, and a few more. Each call site is one line
(`tipMoment('question:saved')`) in an organism or page.

**Tips.** Each tip (`copy/tips.ts`, data like `copy/help.ts`) has:
`id`, the moments it answers, the **skill** it teaches (e.g.
`drag-to-domain`), an optional prerequisite skill, text in Help's inline
tokens (`{{key:…}}`, `{{gesture:…}}`, `{{ui:…}}`, `**…**`), an optional
figure, and the Help topic *More in Help* opens.

**Knowing — graded, never a switch** (the user's correction at approval:
"it should not just disappear once the user has done it one time; it lowers
the chance … an absolute number of times is good, but not the best").
Each skill has **evidence**, a number that grows with use and fades without
it, and knowledge is a smooth function of it:

```
evidence  ← evidence · 2^(−Δdays / 21) + w        // on each signal; fades by half in 3 weeks unused
knowledge = 1 − e^(−evidence / 3)                  // 0 … 1, never exactly 1
```

The signals and their weight `w`: **doing it** 1 — the handlers already
marked `// gesture: <id>` (beta.10) report `tipLearned('<id>')`, as do a
handful of actions (an import, a share, a workspace made); **reading a tip
about it to the end** 0.4; ***Got it*** on it 0.6. So one use lowers the
chance by about a quarter, five uses by about 80 %, and a skill used every
week stays known while one unused for months comes back into play. Nothing
is retired by a count.

**Dismissed.** Closing a tip early (× or Esc) is a different signal — *not
now*, not *I know this* — and it lowers **that tip's** own weight:
`weight ← weight · 0.5`, recovering by half towards 1 every 14 days. A tip
dismissed again and again fades out on its own, and can still come back
much later.

**Small enough is none.** A tip whose chance at a moment is under 2 % is
not drawn at all, so a well-known skill's tips stop appearing in practice
without a rule that says *never*.

**The chance.** At a moment, the candidates are the tips that answer it,
whose prerequisite skill has some knowledge (over 0.25 — one use), and that were not shown in
the last few minutes. If any is left, the engine shows the **one** with the
highest weight, with probability

```
p = novelty × relevance × weight(tip) × (1 − knowledge(skill)) × quiet
novelty   = 0.15 + 0.85 · e^(−experience / 12)      // experience: active days used
relevance = the tip's own weight at this moment (1 for its main moment, less elsewhere)
quiet     = 0 within 90 s of the last tip; otherwise rising to 1 over 5 minutes
```

and a per-day cap that shrinks with experience (6 on the first day, 1 by
the third week). First launch is the exception: *New here? Here's how the
game plays* (opens Help ▸ How the game plays) shows with certainty.

All numbers are constants in one place, named, and covered by tests that
run the engine over simulated weeks (a new operator sees many tips, an
experienced one almost none; nothing repeats within the quiet window).

## How long it stays

`duration = clamp(5 s + words / 3.3 per second + 2 s per badge or figure, 8 s, 30 s)` — reading at ~200 words a minute, time
to look at the visuals, never more than 30 s (§6.4). The clock **pauses**
while the pointer is over the tip or focus is in it. A thin progress line
shows the time left. *Dismiss* (×, or Esc while it has focus) closes it
early.

## How it looks

One new organism, `TipCard`, made of what Studio already has (no new card
style — memory rule, beta.10): a `Panel` (HeroUI `Surface`), a lightbulb
icon and the title, the body through `HelpText` (key caps, gesture badges,
labels in weight), an optional `HelpFigure`, and two quiet actions: *More in
Help* (deep link to the topic) and *Got it*. **No backdrop, no dimming, no
focus taken** (§6.7): `role="status"`, `aria-live="polite"`. It enters with
`motion/react` (Studio chrome only), bottom-right above the status bar, one
at a time; a moment that meets a toast in that corner waits for it once
(as built, P25.2), and while a dialog is open no tip is shown — one already
up holds its clock.

## Where it is remembered

Per operator, in the chrome store (`localStorage`, as list views and
preferences are): shown counts, dismissals, skill knowledge, active days.
Tips are chrome — nothing in them is game truth, so the Zustand rule holds.
**Settings ▸ Settings ▸ Help & tips**: *Show tips* (on by default) and
*Start tips over*.

## Decisions (approved 2026-10-28)

All five as recommended: bottom-right above the status bar; per operator;
learning by doing — **graded**, as above, not a switch; *Show tips* + *Start
tips over*; *How the game plays* certain on first launch.

## Decisions as offered

1. **Placement**: bottom-right above the status bar (recommended), or
   bottom-left by the sidebar?
2. **Remembered per operator** (recommended — two people sharing a laptop
   learn separately) or per install?
3. **Learning by doing**: a gesture used retires its tip (recommended), or
   only dismissals and reads count?
4. **Settings**: *Show tips* + *Start tips over* (recommended), or also a
   *Fewer / Normal / More* choice?
5. **First launch** shows *How the game plays* with certainty (recommended),
   or leave even that to chance?
