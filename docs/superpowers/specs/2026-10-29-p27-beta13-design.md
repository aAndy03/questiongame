# P27 — beta.13: Control and Screens, tweaked (design)

Status: **open** (the maintainer, 2026-10-29: "start beta 13 as open. do not close it"). Decisions here are mine where the maintainer said "continue without expecting my approvals"; each is marked *(my call)*.

Sources: the maintainer's beta.13 brief; the Control audit and the Screens audit (session of 2026-10-29); `docs/research/2026-10-29-display-filters.md`; a run of the real dev app (the Timeline's "2 events" over "No events yet" is real, not a `?fake` artefact).

## Intent

Do **not** redesign Control or Screens. Tweak them: understandability at a glance, ease of use, vertical and horizontal space. Keep what every function already does; fix the bugs found on the way. Three things are new on purpose: the Timeline becomes a fixed horizontal track, the Viewer becomes a 16:9 frame with render quality, and Stage gains display aids chosen from what operating systems ship.

**Anti-goals:** a new layout language; a second writer of play-state; invented filters; hiding a disabled control (it stays, with its reason).

## 1. Token clarity (Control) — "confusing when to change the token"

The rule is unchanged (§5.4): a correct answer keeps the token unless the cap is reached; a wrong answer or the cap frees it; passing early is an override. What changes is that Control *says* so at every step.

1. **The verdict stays.** In `resolved` **and** `handover` the Action Panel keeps a one-line result header: verdict, points, bonus (`outcome.bonusAwarded`, `SESSION.bonus`), and *why the token moved*: "Correct · +120 · Red keeps the token", "Wrong · +0 · The token is free", "Correct · +120 · 3 in a row — cap reached, the token is free".
2. **What next, in words.** `resolved`: under *Next question*, `SESSION.nextQuestionHint` (today unused). `handover`: "Give it to **Blue** (next in order) — press T, or press another team." from `defaultNextTeamId`.
3. **Passing is optional, and looks it.** In `resolved` the ring header reads "Pass the token (optional)" and the holder's row "Keeps the token". In `handover` it reads "Give the token".
4. **The ring block never vanishes** while the Play is open (refuse mode after Lock hid it). `showGiveToken` only chooses between legal and disabled-with-reason.
5. **T is never silent and never a surprise.** In `resolved`, T **arms** ("Pass to Blue? Press T again") and the second T passes; in `handover` T gives at once (the free token has to go somewhere). In a live question in `allow` mode T arms `reveal_and_pass`; elsewhere T toasts the reason it did nothing.
6. **The Viewer agrees with the Action Panel.** A team row during a live question is disabled with `SESSION.revealFirst` (refuse) or arms `reveal_and_pass` (allow), never a plain `give_token` the host refuses.
7. **Round progress** in the Game panel: "Round 2 · 1 of 4 played". *(my call)* The label waits for the last team's turn of a round to resolve before it says the next round, so it doesn't jump early. The host's `roundIndex` is unchanged.
8. **One team (rehearsal):** the free token isn't drawn as held; in `handover` the row reads "Take the token again"; in `resolved` there is no pass block.
9. **Chord hints are the operator's chords:** one `useChord(command)` hook (settings overrides over `HOTKEYS.defaults`) for every `kbd=` in Control.

## 2. Timeline — a fixed horizontal track

The maintainer: "should not even be resizable … all the components that make it need rework; track from left to right."

- **Out of the mosaic.** A fixed strip (`h-[88px]`, full width) under the mosaic, never resized, dragged, stacked or collapsed. Saved layouts that still hold `'timeline'` are pruned on read (persist v2 migration); `timelineCollapsed` goes.
- **A track, left to right, newest at the right**, auto-scrolled to the end, horizontal scroll only (wheel scrolls it sideways).
- **Grouped by turn.** One **turn card** per question: "Q3 · History · Red" over a row of small step dots (revealed, answers, selection, timer, lock), ending in the verdict chip ("Correct +120" / "Wrong"). `token_given` is a small "→ Blue" marker between cards; lifecycle events (started, paused, resumed, restarted, completed, setting changed, reordered, place revealed) are small markers. Every element has its time and detail in a tooltip.
- **Undo is drawn as the host does it:** every event in an active inversion span `from_seq..to_seq` (the same fold as `active_inversions`) is dimmed and struck, with an "Undone" tag on the card; redo restores it.
- **Never "No events yet" over a count.** Loading shows a skeleton row, a failure shows the error; the log is fetched whenever a Play is loaded (no collapsed state left to skip it).
- **Undo / Redo live here, once**, at the strip's right end, with reasons ("Nothing to undo yet", "Redo is only available right after an undo", the overlay wait). *(my call)* They leave the Action Panel's transport, which keeps Restart and Abandon, so the Action Panel has more room. The chords are unchanged.
- The `count <= 2` warning goes.

## 3. Viewer — 16:9, render quality, display aids

- **A 16:9 frame in a still-resizable tile.** The frame fills the tile's width or height (whichever binds). The **slack band** (below if the tile is tall, beside it if the tile is wide) holds the Viewer toolbar. With no slack the toolbar collapses to one "Display" button in the corner, which opens it as a popover. The "NN%" label moves into the toolbar.
- **Render quality: Native · High · Medium · Low** (100 / 75 / 50 / 33 % of the displayed pixels). The iframe is laid out at that fraction and scaled up with a CSS transform; hit targets stay in displayed coordinates. Medium and Low also thin the Viewer's clock posts (to 200 ms and 500 ms). Chrome, per operator (Zustand): a preference, not a Play field. Stage is untouched.
- **Display aids, on demand, live on Stage and in the Viewer.** From the research, and only that set:
  - **Colours:** Normal · Invert (`invert(1) hue-rotate(180deg)`, which keeps hues roughly while flipping lightness) · Greyscale (`grayscale(1)`) · Greyscale inverted.
  - **Dim:** 50–100 % (`brightness()`), for glare and light sensitivity.
  - **Reduce motion:** GSAP timelines jump to their end (`motionConfig.immediate`) and the decor bodies settle and stop. Results are still shown, and `anim-complete` is still reported once.
  - Not included: colour-blindness "correction" (no published matrices, weak evidence), and high contrast or larger text. Stage is already 21:1 black on white with very large type, and those need tokens or layout, not filters.
- **Truth is on the host.** New Control-behaviour globals `stage.view.filter`, `stage.view.brightness`, `stage.view.reduce_motion`, written with `settings_set_global` (live mid-Play, never frozen). They reach Stage in a new `PlayStateForStage.view`, as audio does. Stage applies them to its root and never decides them itself. Being settings, they persist; *(my call)* a teacher who needs them every lesson shouldn't have to set them again.

## 4. Game, Action Panel, Strips — small tweaks

- **Game panel:** round progress (§1.7); the holder is scrolled into view; the domain footer shows the domain in play and "x / y left"; `CHROME_HEIGHT` is measured, not guessed; the overlooked styling is the Action Panel's (the default team only).
- **Action Panel:**
  - **Reveal outcome before Lock arms** ("Not locked — counts as wrong. Press again"), on the button, R and the Viewer target. Timeout reveals are unchanged.
  - **Option buttons:** a digit beside the letter ("1 · A").
  - **Domain buttons:** digit hints.
  - **Disabled reasons:** paused → "Resume the Play first"; operator pace shown where the clock would be; the hard-coded 'Auto' moved into copy ("Stage reveals this by itself").
  - **Ring rows:** trailing text goes to the tooltip below comfortable.
  - **Layout:** the transport keeps Restart and Abandon, with Abandon set apart.
- **Strips:**
  - **Overlay buttons:** disabled with a reason when their payload would lie ("No outcome yet", "Nobody holds the token", "No answer selected").
  - **Screens:** a screen index ("3 / 7") and `[` `]` chord hints.
  - **Overlay pills:** each kind gets its own icon.
- **Default split** *(my call)*: game 18 / viewer 50 / action+strips 32, so the Action column isn't compact at 1100 px. Only the default changes; saved layouts stay.
- **Chords respect the panel:** a chord whose button is disabled does nothing, and while a dialog is open the chords are off.

## 5. Screens — rework within its design

**Bugs:**

- **The canvas shows the recipe's decor.** `previewPlay` carries `layout.decor`.
- **Decor moves on the canvas.** A canvas-only `simulate` flag (`/stage/?viewer&hold&simulate`) runs the frame loop, because Screens is never open during a show. A *Motion* switch on the canvas toolbar pauses it.
- **Overrides seed from Stage.** Override anchor point and boundary start from the values Stage reports, so turning an override on moves nothing.
- **Apply to all screens** skips slots whose key kind differs (team id against rank) and says "Applied to N screens".
- **Number steppers.** An empty stepper starts from 0 for offsets and from the measured size for size and font. A first press no longer jumps to −1920 or 1 px.
- **Save state.** "All changes saved", "Saving…" from the first edit, and "Not saved" with *Retry*. The revision number moves to a tooltip.
- **Loading.** A skeleton while the games list or the game loads, never "No games yet".
- **Arrow keys** nudge only the selected node. Escape clears the selection. A click on empty Stage (inside the iframe) deselects.
- **Preview fixes.** The *Detail label* uses team notes in the preview. The preview timer follows the game's timer (hidden at operator pace).
- **`anchorPhase`** uses `STEP_HZ`.
- **Current team** says "This screen has no decor shapes" and drops the per-screen decor block.

**Anchor indicator (the maintainer's example):**

- Stage adds `anchor` and `boundary` (frame pixels) to its `nodes` message.
- The canvas draws a crosshair at the anchor and a dashed rectangle for the boundary. They show while the Decor panel has focus or hover, and always while an override is on.
- The crosshair can be dragged to write the anchor.

**Editability (the maintainer's example, "Built in"):**

- **The empty Inspector** says "Click any word or block on the screen to change its words, size, colour or place". Below that, a list of this screen's elements, each a button that selects it.
- **Handles** show a faint outline whenever the canvas is hovered. Elements with editable words carry an "Aa" badge. Text overrides count as changed.
- **Stage reports what it draws.** It adds each node's text and computed font size to the `nodes` message. The Inspector shows "Now: PLAYING SEQUENCE" and "Built-in: 48 px" in place of the placeholder "Built-in", so the value is measured and the design-system boundary still holds.
- **Readable names.** The Inspector reads "Question ▸ Question text" or "Team order ▸ Row · Red". The id is kept as a small mono caption.
- **Shared words say so:** "(every header)", "(Question and Current team)", "(every row)". A row's style fields say "This team's row only".
- **Language.** A local EN | RO switch in the Text section changes what the preview shows and which language you edit. The game's language is unchanged.
- **Inspector order:** the element's name first, then Text, then Style.

**Dials:**

- **Units:** px/s, °/s, seconds, % of the boundary.
- **Captions:** "Drift above Max speed has no effect", and attract = repel = 0 turns the anchor off.
- **Disabled reasons:** "Needs the Collide + attract mode" and "Turn Anchor on".
- **Grouping:** "Anchor timing (every screen)" is separate from "This screen ▸ Boundary box / Anchor point".
- **Reset:** a *Built-in decor* reset in the Decor panel header.

**Layout:**

- **Tabs:** the seven screens, then a captioned "Overlays" group of five without the "Overlay ·" prefix. The selected tab scrolls into view.
- **One count per screen** covers nodes, the box and this screen's text, drawn as a Chip.
- **The aside** gets *Inspector | Decor* tabs. The resets move into the Inspector's footer. The boundary is a 2×2 grid.
- **Live chip:** "On Stage now" when this game is the one loaded in Control. The `previewHint` caption goes under the canvas.
- **Undo:** Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z over a session-local draft history, with Undo and Redo buttons beside the save state.
- **Drag:** the handle and an x/y readout follow the pointer while you drag.

**The tip card** no longer covers the right-hand panels. On Screens and Control it sits above the status bar, at the left edge of the main column *(my call)*.
